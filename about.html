Our Origin

Our Story of Hospitality

Dream Gurkhas Inn is a labor of love, founded on the values of family and the legendary hospitality of our heritage. What began as a dream to share the serenity of our mountain home has grown into a cherished haven for families seeking connection and comfort. Our architecture reflects this journey, blending traditional motifs with warm, welcoming spaces designed for all generations.

As a family-run resort, every detail is personal. We invite you to become part of our extended family and experience a stay where safety, warmth, and shared memories define every moment.
